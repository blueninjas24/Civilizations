Actual staffed farms can split future wheat work between basic food and separate
public grain stock. Only wheat's observed share of the planted footprint qualifies;
complete typed evidence and actual productive farm work are required. Unknown,
partial, zero-wheat, unstaffed or paused-world inputs cannot create grain. Old food
and all historical food periods remain untouched.

Default editable settings in newly generated `economy.yml`:

```yaml
grain-harvest:
  enabled: true
  grain-share-percent: 25
  protected-food-periods: 2
  storage-capacity: 256
  grain-units-per-harvest: 1
```

Missing this section in installed files disables new harvesting without deleting
anything. Files are never rewritten. Finish already earned food work under old
settings before changing terms; the existing forward tuning audit records changes.

At most the configured percentage of wheat's share of the same harvest work is
diverted. It must also fit finite grain storage and leave enough available food
for this period's actual meals plus configured future reserve periods. Opening
food, genuinely due deliveries and this harvest contribute to that availability.
No old food is converted. Example: eight work units from all-wheat fields can
produce six food and two grain when food reserves allow it. If food is tight or
grain storage full, the work remains basic food. Yield, allocation, reserve and
storage are independently editable prototype terms.

Schema72 transactionally adds initially empty `grain_stocks` and `grain_harvests`.
The food period, actual grain allocation, stock and checkpoint commit together.
History retains total work, food output, diverted work, grain yield/stock,
observed wheat/total crops, food protection and policy/explanation. Late failure
rolls back everything. Replay cannot repeat a harvest. Capacity reduction retains
existing grain and stops additional grain until space is available; changing yield
affects only future work. Server downtime and absent worlds earn no grain.

Tool service and farming experience use the same actual harvest work, including
grain-only work. Grain does not add workers or make tools/skill free. Legacy
`food` period identifiers in those existing work logs identify the shared economic
period; new explanations say harvest work, and grain history explains the split.
Admin food diagnostics show grain stored/harvested and basic food produced when
the current period has a saved grain allocation checkpoint.
