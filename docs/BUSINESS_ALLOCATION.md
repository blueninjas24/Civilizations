# Shared business priorities

The user approved urgent village needs first, then unmet demand from real funded
customers, with editable priorities and fair rotation on ties. This first verified
connection applies to vacancies at existing non-farm businesses and selection among
viable resource and marker startup adapters. Existing farms also receive the urgent-food
scheduling hook below; animal startup comparison remains next work.
No worker is transferred, no wage wallet is created and no living-owner firm closes.

New-file `business-allocation` in economy.yml enables the comparison. Missing old
sections opt out; installed files are never overwritten. Set `urgent-first`,
`urgent-industries`, `review-millis` and `maximum-demand-value-hundredths`. Defaults:
urgent farms/bakeries/fisheries when the latest saved food assessment has unmet meals,
5-second reviews, and1,000,000 maximum reference-value hundredths per candidate.
When enabled, an existing farm with complete saved fields and an actual unmet-meal
assessment gets a bounded staffing pass before other vacancies. The assessment
must still match current resident needs; the world must be present and the saved
farm must be operating. This uses its existing crop-limited qualifications/capacity,
never creates a new farm, advances time, produces food, pays money or moves jobs.
At most two villages are visited per pulse. Incomplete fields, absent worlds and
changed population wait. Active students are excluded from farm hiring as well.

Ordinary input-supply and village-purchase unit prices value funded shortages;
coal quantities therefore do not automatically outweigh rarer goods. The default
automatic-staffing catalog now also includes bakeries, craftsmen and livestock.

One village per bounded runtime visit is compared completely: up to32 firms,
512 idle residents and512 saved assignments. Unknown worlds, incomplete demand or
oversized comparisons defer; no partial group is treated as complete. Actual jobs
require a living local owner, a real vacancy and an age/skill-qualified idle adult.
Active students are excluded consistently with the diagnostic report. Exhausted
or untrusted finite sources and forests without habitat are excluded. Current
owned/incoming goods and actual customer funds affect the existing demand review.
Firms with neither funded demand nor configured urgency cannot claim a worker.

The student reservation belongs to shared allocation. Disabled/missing shared
settings retain legacy hiring, including a trainee who has already earned the
actual job requirement; the existing study loop then cancels that employed
resident's course. Enabling shared priorities waits until study releases them.
Neither mode skips the saved job's age/skill checks or grants extra experience.
Shared selection diagnostics name the village and selected business explicitly,
so a result shown alongside a bounded page is not mistaken for every firm's hire.

Urgency is the first tier; bounded funded shortage value is second. Equal priorities
rotate after the last selected business. A bounded candidate page still determines
which qualified person can be hired; it can take another turn to reach a late
candidate. This is not a guarantee that every firm gains staff or stays profitable.
Hiring does not itself spend the customer's money or reserve a sale.

Resource investment now compares the current viable proposal from each of the
mining, quarry/sandpit and logging adapters. A fresh suitable resource, qualified
idle founder, actual funded customer, feasible warehouse chain, existing-capacity
check and protected grant eligibility are required before a proposal can compete.
The adapter's existing bounded patch selection remains in place; this compares
industries rather than claiming exhaustive ranking of every possible ore patch.
Public demand is recalculated after each proposed startup debit, so its own grant
cannot also count as money still available for village purchases. Food-budget
guards still prohibit grants during unmet-meal assessments.

One initialized village is visited per owner pulse, with the configured review
interval per village. The selected adapter repeats its normal validation and
creates at most one business. Schema98 adds empty `resource_investment_state` and
`resource_investment_reviews`; the actual grant, business, first job, source identity,
selection audit and tie cursor commit together. Refused/stale opportunities create
no approval or cursor advancement. Founder study continues through the existing
economy system; comparison does not award skills or buy practice supplies.

Qualified blacksmith, warehouse, bakery and craftsman preparations now join the
same comparison, one proposal per enabled marker adapter. Workshops use their
prepared recipe's outputs; warehouses can supply their admitted storage catalog.
The four existing preparation methods retain their actual funding, skills, inputs,
customer and marker-evidence checks. Comparison adds no course or reservation.
If a marker wins, the owner pulse waits for its normal Paper adapter to confirm
the already-loaded point. Unknown/unloaded or removed markers cannot spend funds.
Acceptance compares again immediately before normal startup revalidation.

The actual grant, stock/cargo, first job and selection audit/cursor commit in that
startup's existing transaction. Audit failure rolls everything back. Saved exact
startup replays bypass a new comparison and do not add another selection record.
The existing schema98 ledger also stores these marker kinds; no migration, row
rewrite or renamed table. A high-priority provisional marker can temporarily delay
other startups until confirmed or its existing observation expires; this never
loads a chunk to obtain proof. Selection does not guarantee a completed future sale.

Fisheries now contribute one qualified funded water opportunity as well. A winning
water proposal waits for the existing Paper shoreline/water confirmation. Its
priority key identifies the saved water footprint; the actual bank-specific
business/site identity is unchanged. The normal acceptance revalidates current
water, qualified founder, funding and the safe bank before committing its selection
audit with the grant, job, habitat and empty store. A later bank relocation or
restart neither changes that historical selection nor grants another startup.

Explicitly disabling private automatic input purchases leaves public food demand
available to the selector and existing-business staffing. This contributes no
private demand and does not treat an incomplete enabled review as complete. Public
orders still require their own real funds and matching current saved assessment.

Schema97 adds initially empty `business_allocation_state` and
`business_allocation_reviews`. Hiring, its existing audit, selection explanation and
saved tie cursor commit together. Failure rolls back all of them. Review throttling
also covers complete comparisons with no candidate, without adding empty historical
rows on every pulse. The report remains read-only; business diagnostics show the
configured selection result. Marker-confirmed workshops/warehouses retain their
existing acceptance adapters with the shared choice now connected. Fishing uses
its existing bank confirmation with the same ledger. Livestock adoption does not
yet share this startup comparison; do not call the whole
specialization/competition gate done.
